About

From asking why to measuring how much.

I started in philosophy and sociology, which taught me to ask who a system actually serves. Health economics gave me the tools to answer it with numbers. I'm now completing an MSc in Health Economics and Policy at the University of Lucerne, working across economic evaluation, health policy, and quantitative methods.

What I keep coming back to is the gap between the data and the person. Switzerland publishes a complete, public list of every reimbursed medicine and its price — and almost nobody outside the system can read it. Most of my projects start there: take something official and opaque, and turn it into something a person can use.

Live app

SLCheck — Swiss medication coverage

A lookup tool for the Swiss Spezialitätenliste. Search any medicine by name, active substance, ATC code or GTIN and get its reimbursement status, the patient's co-payment, the official limitation text, and the cheapest generic alternative with the saving calculated. Built as a prototype, in German, French, Italian and English, and installable to a phone home screen.
