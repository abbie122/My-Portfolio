About

From asking why
to measuring how much.

Philosophy taught me to ask who a system serves. Health economics gave me the tools to answer that with numbers. Code is how I get to the numbers, and how I check them.

Switzerland publishes a full public list of every medicine its insurance covers and what each one costs. Almost nobody outside the system can read it. That is where most of my projects start: take something official and hard to follow, and turn it into something a person can use.

Live tool

SLCheck — Swiss medication coverage

A search tool for the official Swiss medicines list. Look up any medicine and see whether insurance covers it, how much the patient pays, any restrictions on prescribing it, and the cheapest generic with the saving worked out. In four languages, and it installs to a phone home screen.

Analysis

What the medicines list reveals

An analysis of all 10,405 medicines covered by Swiss mandatory insurance: how prices are spread, how cost relates to prescribing restrictions, and how unevenly generic competition reaches different areas of medicine. Including one hypothesis that didn't survive testing, and one headline figure I had to go back and correct.
